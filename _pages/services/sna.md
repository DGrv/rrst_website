<h1>🎽 Bib Collection</h1>

<h2>🎯 Goals</h2>

<ul>
    <li>📱 Make our tools simple and intuitive for event helpers.</li>
    <li>🔧 Let helpers take responsibility for the devices they use.</li>
    <li>🔄 Give helpers the flexibility to adapt to changes during the event.</li>
    <li>💳 Allow new participants to register and pay on site.</li>
    <li>⚡ Keep the process fast and efficient to avoid queues.</li>
</ul>

<h2>🚀 How</h2>

<ul>
    <li>
        <strong>📱 Use the helpers' own smartphones</strong><br>
        Smartphones make deployment simple and maintenance easy. Helpers already have:
        <ul>
            <li>🌐 Internet access</li>
            <li>🔋 A power cable or charger</li>
        </ul>
    </li>

    <li>
        <strong>🔗 Direct access through the Kiosk</strong><br>
        The device connects directly to our database through the <strong>Kiosk</strong>,
        providing fast and secure access to participant information.
    </li>

    <li>
        <strong>🔎 Quick participant lookup</strong><br>
        The Kiosk allows helpers to quickly find a participant, see their bib number,
        and, when necessary, update selected information.
    </li>

    <li>
        <strong>📝 Flexible registration</strong><br>
        Registration remains in the participant's hands:
        <ul>
            <li>🌐 Registration is always available online.</li>
            <li>💳 Payment can be made online or on site.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>

    <li>
        <strong>📲 Fast access with QR codes</strong><br>
        QR codes make the process even faster:
        <ul>
            <li>📷 Scan to access the registration page.</li>
            <li>✉️ Scan the QR code from the confirmation email to quickly access the participant's information.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
</ul>
